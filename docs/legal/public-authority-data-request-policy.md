# Roobs World Limited public-authority data request policy

**Status:** Draft for director approval  
**Owner:** Roobs World Limited  
**Applies to:** Requests from public authorities for personal data controlled by Roobs World Limited, including Meta Platform Data processed for Riddle Quest

## Required review

Roobs World Limited will not disclose personal data merely because a public authority asks for it. Before any disclosure, the company will record the request and review:

- the identity and authority of the requester;
- the legal basis, jurisdiction, scope and binding effect of the request;
- whether the request was served through the required legal process;
- whether notice to the affected person is permitted or required; and
- whether disclosure would conflict with applicable UK law, contractual duties or another binding obligation.

The company will seek qualified legal advice when the validity, scope, jurisdiction or available response is unclear.

## Challenging unlawful or excessive requests

Roobs World Limited will reject, narrow, seek clarification of, or challenge a request when there are reasonable grounds to consider it unlawful, invalid, overbroad, disproportionate or outside the requesting authority's jurisdiction. The company will use an available appeal, objection or review process where doing so is lawful and proportionate.

## Data minimisation

If disclosure is legally required, Roobs World Limited will disclose only the minimum data necessary to comply with the valid request. The company will limit the covered people, fields, date range and copies wherever the request and applicable law permit. Credentials, secrets and unrelated records will not be included.

## Documentation and security

For each request, the company will retain a restricted record of:

- the request and requester;
- dates received, reviewed and answered;
- the legal basis and review outcome;
- any clarification, objection or challenge;
- the data disclosed or the reason no data was disclosed;
- the people who approved and performed the response; and
- any notice given to the affected person.

Only authorised personnel may access a request record or prepare a disclosure. Data must be transferred through an appropriately secure method. Retention of the request record will follow applicable legal, security and accountability requirements.

## Emergency requests and confidentiality

An asserted emergency does not remove the identity, authority, legality and minimisation checks. The company will handle a lawful confidentiality requirement according to its terms and will reassess whether notice can be given when the restriction expires.

## Review

The director will review this policy after a relevant legal or operational change and at least annually. Adoption must be recorded with an effective date before the company represents this draft as an active policy in a provider questionnaire.
