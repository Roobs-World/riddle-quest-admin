## Information used to provide the game

We receive a Facebook game-scoped identifier and signed authentication information to sign you into your game account. Our services keep your game profile, chosen game name, progress, guesses and results, coin balance, achievements and preferences. We use Facebook game storage and browser storage for supported preferences and restoration of play. We do not request your Facebook password, and the game does not import Facebook names or photos into its ordinary game profile. When supported, Meta can display its own profile overlay on a designated social surface.

Where social features are enabled, we process participation, results, game identities and relationships needed for challenges, rankings and rival interactions. Presence is optional and shows coarse states such as online or playing to eligible rivals. Blocks restrict interactions and presence visibility. Player reports identify the reporting and reported game accounts and a selected report category. This version does not provide voice or text chat or arbitrary avatar uploads.

Where Facebook purchases are enabled, Meta handles checkout. Our services verify the purchase information needed to deliver coins or an entitlement and prevent duplicate grants, and process refund records. We do not collect your full card details. In-game coins are game benefits, distinct from the currency shown at Meta checkout. The release catalog consists of fixed coin packs and permanent removal of interstitial advertisements; these are not randomized purchases.

We also process service requests, security records, technical errors and correspondence you send to support. Please do not send passwords, authentication codes or full payment details. Riddle generation uses OpenAI services; generation requests concern puzzle content rather than a player's Facebook profile.

## Providers and international processing

Meta provides the host platform, platform account and supported payment and social services. Firebase and Google Cloud provide game authentication, databases, functions, storage and operational logs. OpenAI supplies puzzle generation. Sentry may receive minimized diagnostic events when configured. Support correspondence uses the publisher's Gmail mailbox. Provider access is limited to the relevant service purpose and contractual arrangements; players should also consult the host platform's own privacy information.

The game does not operate solely within the UK or EU: the current database is in Europe, while Facebook identity and gameplay functions also run in the United States. Applicable provider contracts and transfer safeguards still need verification for review readiness. The public notice directs players to contact the publisher for information about applicable arrangements.

## Retention and deletion

An active game account retains its progress and benefits until it is deleted or an approved retention rule applies. Use in-game account deletion, Meta's available deletion controls, or the support address to request help. We may need to verify that a request concerns your account.

Deletion removes or minimizes the game records covered by the deletion process. It does not delete your Facebook account, Meta's payment records, recipient copies of shared material, or every provider security log or backup. Limited server-only identity and deletion records remain so a later deliberate fresh start is separate from the deleted account and old requests cannot act on the new account. Purchase and reward replay records may also remain to prevent duplicated benefits. These are retained records, not anonymous data or a promise of complete erasure.

Current infrastructure backup settings retain daily database backups for seven days and weekly backups for 28 days; deleted storage objects have a seven-day recovery period. Default operational logging is configured for 30 days and required audit logging for 400 days. Backups and logs are distinct from live account records; expiry settings do not prove a particular record has already been removed.

Routine resolved player reports and support correspondence: **owner approved 90 days after closure on 19 September 2026; implementation pending**, with documented exceptions for active disputes or legal obligations. Identity/deletion safeguards, commerce records, telemetry, provider records and any unresolved safety case need an approved retention basis and verified deletion process before release. Do not publish these editorial qualifications as a finished policy.

## Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal information, object to qualifying processing and request portability where applicable. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Contact grindheadgames@gmail.com; your available rights depend on the processing and applicable law. You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk/ or your relevant supervisory authority. Separate requests about a Facebook account or Meta-held payment information may need to go to Meta.
