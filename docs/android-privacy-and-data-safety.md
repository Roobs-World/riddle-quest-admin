# Android privacy notice and Data safety draft

Status: prepared for owner review on 20 November 2026. This document describes the Android branch's implementation. It has not been published as a privacy notice or submitted to Play Console. No Google Play developer account or internal-testing release exists yet. The final store declaration must match the signed release and the operator's actual retention settings.

## Privacy notice draft

Roobs World Limited operates Riddle Quest. Contact grindheadgames@gmail.com about game data and privacy requests. This notice covers the Android app, application id `com.roobsworld.riddlequest`.

The app starts with an anonymous Firebase account to save online progress. If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Firebase process the credentials and provider account information needed to link that account, which may include its identifier, name, email and profile information. The game continues to use your chosen game name and selected game avatar. Our game services store a game account identifier, a chosen game name, an optional selected profile image, language, progress, guesses and results, coin balance, achievements and multiplayer records. Game names, selected profile images and ranking results can be visible to other players in the game's profile, leaderboard and race views. Sharing an invitation uses the Android share sheet, which lets you choose its destination. We do not access your address book. The app uses Android's photo picker for an optional profile image rather than requesting access to your whole photo library.

Offline mode keeps its own progress on this device. It does not transfer offline coins into the online account. App-private storage also keeps sign-in state, preferences and unfinished online answers so the app can recover interrupted play. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes these local records and can lose access to an anonymous account.

Google Play handles purchases. Our backend verifies the product, purchase token, Play order information and subscription status with Google before granting coins or Premium. The app passes a hash of the game account identifier to Play to bind purchases to the right account. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Google manages payment methods, subscriptions and its own transaction records.

Notifications are optional. You can enable race invitations and a Daily reminder separately in the app's notification settings and turn either off again. Android may also ask for notification permission. When enabled, the app sends a Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token, game account identifier, selected notification choices and language to our services. We use them to deliver the selected notifications. The Daily reminder is scheduled for 18:00 UTC. An invitation can contain the inviter's game name and a challenge identifier. Tapping it opens the relevant game view; it does not accept a race or grant any reward automatically.

A shared installation link can pass an invitation or referral entry through Google Play. The app uses Play Install Referrer Library 2.2 to read only the raw referrer string from the Play Store service. It does not read click or installation timestamps, app version or the instant-app flag. The app validates the entry and keeps only the canonical game URL locally, with a receipt to prevent that Play entry from opening again. It does not keep unrelated campaign parameters or send the raw referrer to the game backend. The player must explicitly accept the preview before the game joins a race or binds a referral. Reading the referrer grants no reward and accepts no race.

Referral rewards require optional Google sign-in. The backend verifies the Google subject in Firebase authentication claims and stores a hash of that subject, the current game account binding, and whether that subject has already played or accepted a referral. Referral links contain random, expiring tokens. A new player must accept a token before online play or purchases; rewards follow a server-confirmed first win. Opening the share sheet grants nothing. The inviter has a daily reward cap.

Our services also process requests and technical/security records needed to run the game and prevent abuse. Play Integrity and Firebase App Check help check whether requests come from a recognized app and device. The Android artifact includes no advertising or analytics SDK. Backend service logs and error reporting remain part of the game infrastructure. The app does not collect precise location, contacts, audio, or advertising identifiers.

Firebase and Google Cloud provide authentication, game backend storage and processing, notification delivery, and service security. Google Play provides billing and integrity checks. The service inventory in [Privacy data inventory](privacy-data-inventory.md) describes the wider backend, including operational error reporting. Operator-configured provider retention, regions, support handling, international transfers and applicable privacy-rights wording must be confirmed before this draft is published.

Use Delete account in the profile to request removal of the online game account and related game data. Deletion removes native notification token registrations and pending invitation records, and anonymizes shared multiplayer records where needed to preserve another player's game. Purchase replay-prevention records may be retained without an active account association so a receipt cannot grant rewards twice. Google can retain its own payment and service records. Account deletion does not cancel a Play subscription; use Google Play's subscription management screen to cancel it. A new anonymous account has separate progress.

## Retention and deletion evidence

- Account and game records follow the existing server-authoritative account-deletion pipeline in `profile/accountDeletion`.
- Native registrations are stored under `androidPushTokens/{sha256(token)}`. Each record contains the current uid, FCM token, consent, locale, last refresh and a 30-day expiry. Refresh renews consent already chosen on the device; it never opts in a new account. A token has only its latest authenticated owner and an account can register at most five devices.
- Disabling all notifications deletes the current owner's server registration and immediately prevents device display even if the server revocation fails. The next foreground transition refreshes an enabled registration with the latest SDK token. Provider-invalid tokens are deleted when a send reports them invalid. Stale Daily registrations are removed by the reminder scheduler. The Firestore TTL policy on `androidPushTokens.expiresAt` is ACTIVE to retire other stale registrations too.
- Account deletion removes all registrations whose uid belongs to the deleted account. Native display also checks the active local account and consent before displaying any message. A stale token cannot display another account's invitation after sign-out or deletion.
- Daily dispatch claims its next schedule in a transaction before contacting FCM. Retried scheduler work cannot send the same claimed reminder again. Invitation dispatch uses the existing one-attempt outbox claim. Transport ambiguity is not proof of delivery.
- Android referral deletion removes active tokens and outbox work, clears the account pointer in the durable Google-subject registry, and removes game account identifiers from binding/award records. Minimal pseudonymous replay markers keyed by a hash of the Google subject remain, including prior-play and referral-bound flags, so deleting and recreating an account cannot earn the same acquisition reward again. Daily inviter caps remain keyed by that durable subject across account generations; their expiry is two days. Issuance-rate receipts expire after seven days. The Firestore TTL policies for the token, issuance-rate and daily-cap expiry fields are ACTIVE.
- The installation-scoped entry inbox stores a canonical pending URL and one-time consumed Play-entry receipt in `riddle-quest:android:entry-inbox-v1`. The pending entry survives process restarts until the matching acceptance, explicit dismissal or Back, or a known terminal or unavailable result. Sign-out and account deletion do not remove this installation-scoped envelope. The consumed URL remains after dismissal so rereading the same Play referrer does not reopen it. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the envelope; it has no cross-uninstall guarantee. The app does not persist the raw referrer, click or installation timestamps or unrelated campaign parameters.
- Local save data and account-scoped notification preferences stay in app-private storage until overwritten, cleared or the app is uninstalled. Firebase/Google can retain installation/service records under their own policies; deleting a game account is not a claim that every provider log vanishes immediately.
- Verified Google Play subscription tokens are kept only in the server-only `androidCommerceAccounts/{uid}` registry, with a maximum of 16 renewable or recoverable receipts. Sync verifies both stored tokens and device additions with Play; an empty second-device Play account cannot revoke another device's verified Premium. Expired/not-found receipts are retired after Play verification, and account deletion removes the private raw-token registry. Registry versions prevent stale concurrent verification from overwriting newly registered entitlement.
- Android purchase-grant deletion replaces each linked record at its token-hash document with an identity-free replay tombstone. The tombstone retains provider, replay-protection marker, product, coin amount and grant time; it drops uid and Play order ID. Replay stays refused after deletion. Confirm the operator's financial-record retention before publishing the notice.
