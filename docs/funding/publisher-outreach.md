# Publisher And Investor Outreach

## Target Publisher Profile

Prioritize publishers with:

- HTML5, casual, puzzle, word, trivia, or daily-game experience.
- Soft-launch or user-acquisition capability.
- Comfort with free-to-play or hybrid casual monetization.
- Willingness to fund polish, launch marketing, or UA testing.
- No requirement to take Riddle Quest IP ownership.

Avoid publishers whose portfolio is mainly premium PC/console, web3, gambling, or ad-network-only reskins.

## Publisher Ask

Preferred asks, in order:

1. Marketing and soft-launch support with a modest minimum guarantee.
2. UA testing budget plus production support.
3. Distribution/platform relationships and launch featuring support.
4. Development funding only if IP ownership and sequel rights remain protected.

## Outreach Rules

- Send no more than 10 cold messages per day.
- Track every contact in `outreach-tracker.csv`.
- Follow up once after 5-7 business days, then stop unless they engage.
- Never send inflated metrics. Use "pre-launch beta" until live traction exists.
- Share builds only through controlled distribution links.
- Before signing anything, review IP ownership, sequel rights, recoupment, minimum guarantees, marketing obligations, data access, and termination rights.
