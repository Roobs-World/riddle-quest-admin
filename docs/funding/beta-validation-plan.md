# Closed Beta Validation Plan

## Goal

Recruit 100-300 target players and produce a credible beta metrics report that improves grant, publisher, investor, and launch conversations.

## Audience

Primary testers:

- Browser puzzle players aged 18-45.
- People who play Wordle, crosswords, trivia, anagrams, or daily brain-training games.
- Players comfortable with 5-10 minute daily sessions.

Secondary testers:

- Accessibility-sensitive users.
- Players who are skeptical of freemium monetization.
- Casual players who rarely complete long onboarding flows.

## Recruitment Channels

- Existing personal/professional network.
- Puzzle and word-game communities where self-promotion is allowed.
- Small paid social test focused on puzzle fans.
- App/game beta groups.
- Publisher/platform contacts who can share with internal reviewers.

## Beta Structure

**Phase 1: 25 players**

- Verify onboarding comprehension.
- Catch crashes, responsive-browser issues, identity friction, and unclear puzzle feedback.
- Collect qualitative feedback before scaling.

**Phase 2: 100 players**

- Measure D1 retention, completion rate, average guesses, hint usage, and tutorial completion.
- Identify difficulty spikes and confusing riddles.

**Phase 3: 100-300 players**

- Measure D7 retention, daily challenge return rate, Jest purchase intent, and referral/share interest.
- Collect testimonials and short gameplay clips where permission is granted.

## KPI Dashboard

Track these before beta begins:

- Signups from landing page.
- Guest-to-registered conversion.
- Tutorial start rate.
- Tutorial completion rate.
- First puzzle completion rate.
- Daily Challenge start and completion rate.
- Classic Mode level completion rate.
- Average guesses per solved puzzle.
- Failed puzzle rate.
- Hint usage rate.
- Coin balance before and after hint use.
- Jest coin purchase intent and purchase conversion if enabled.
- D1 retention.
- D7 retention.
- Crash-free sessions.
- Accessibility or readability complaints.

## Survey

Use a 2-minute post-session survey:

1. What made you want to keep playing?
2. What made you want to stop?
3. Was the riddle difficulty too easy, about right, or too hard?
4. Did the color feedback make sense?
5. Did hints feel fair at 50 coins?
6. Would you consider buying a coin pack through Jest?
7. Did the shop prices and coin value feel clear?
8. Which mode would you return for: Daily Challenge, Classic Mode, both, or neither?
9. What one thing should be improved before launch?
10. May we quote your feedback anonymously in funding materials?

## Beta Report Template

The funding-ready beta report should include:

- Date range and player count.
- Acquisition channels.
- Device/platform split.
- Top-level funnel.
- Retention summary.
- Puzzle completion and difficulty summary.
- Monetization intent summary.
- Top five qualitative insights.
- Product changes already made.
- Product changes planned next.
- Funding implication: what the data proves and what remains risky.
