# Riddle Quest Pitch Deck Outline

Deck length: 10 slides.  
Audience: grant panels, HTML5/casual publishers, angels, and platform contacts.
Tone: commercially grounded, player-first, evidence-led.

## Slide 1 - Cover

**Kicker:** RIDDLE QUEST  
**Claim:** A daily word puzzle becomes a mini-mystery.  
**Proof object:** Game logo/key art plus three proof points: Jest beta, fair freemium, scalable content.
**Speaker note:** Riddle Quest is a pre-launch HTML5 puzzle game on Jest combining riddle solving with Wordle-style feedback and long-term progression.

## Slide 2 - Problem

**Kicker:** PLAYER FATIGUE  
**Claim:** Word puzzles need context, not just grids.  
**Proof object:** Comparison strip: standard word guessing vs riddle-led deduction.  
**Speaker note:** Many word games become pattern exercises. Riddle Quest gives each answer a narrative clue, making each puzzle feel distinct.

## Slide 3 - Product

**Kicker:** CORE LOOP  
**Claim:** Read the riddle, test a guess, learn from feedback, solve the mystery.  
**Proof object:** Four-step gameplay flow: Riddle -> Guess -> Feedback -> Reward.  
**Speaker note:** The loop is familiar enough for instant onboarding but different enough to stand out.

## Slide 4 - Modes And Retention

**Kicker:** DAILY + PROGRESSION  
**Claim:** Daily habit and classic progression support different session lengths.  
**Proof object:** Two-lane product map: Daily Challenge for repeat visits; Classic Mode for unlock-driven progression.  
**Speaker note:** Players can dip in for one daily puzzle or continue through sequential levels.

## Slide 5 - Differentiation

**Kicker:** WHY THIS ONE  
**Claim:** Riddle Quest adds content variety, persistence, and scalable production to the word-game formula.  
**Proof object:** Differentiator matrix: riddle context, variable answer lengths, server validation, AI-assisted content, profiles, achievements, cross-platform sync.  
**Speaker note:** The project is not dependent on a static puzzle list.

## Slide 6 - Business Model

**Kicker:** FAIR FREEMIUM  
**Claim:** Monetization supports play without forcing spend.  
**Proof object:** Monetization bridge: free play -> coins from play -> hints -> optional Jest coin packs.
**Speaker note:** The design avoids paywalls and uses opt-in value exchanges.

## Slide 7 - Evidence

**Kicker:** BUILT, NOT JUST DESCRIBED  
**Claim:** The beta already has the technical foundations funders usually ask to see.  
**Proof object:** Proof stack: working React game, Jest SDK, Firebase backend, Jest purchases, achievements, profiles, and automated tests.
**Speaker note:** Funding goes into beta polish and market validation, not proving whether the core product can be built.

## Slide 8 - Funding Ask

**Kicker:** GBP 24.5K ASK  
**Claim:** A focused 90-day sprint turns a working beta into a fundable launch story.  
**Proof object:** Budget donut or stacked bar from `budget-and-runway.csv`.  
**Speaker note:** The ask stays under GBP 30k and concentrates on the work that creates commercial evidence.

## Slide 9 - 90-Day Plan

**Kicker:** VALIDATION SPRINT  
**Claim:** The next milestone is measurable player evidence.  
**Proof object:** Timeline: readiness, applications, beta, publisher push.  
**Speaker note:** Success is defined by applications submitted, beta signups, KPI report, and active partner conversations.

## Slide 10 - Close

**Kicker:** DECISION  
**Claim:** Fund the beta sprint to de-risk launch while preserving the upside of original IP.  
**Proof object:** Three requested outcomes: funding commitment, reviewer build access, intro to HTML5/casual publishers.
**Speaker note:** Riddle Quest is ready for a small, disciplined funding push that can unlock larger opportunities.
