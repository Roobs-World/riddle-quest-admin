# Grant Application Checklist

## Primary Target: UK Games Fund Prototype Fund

Current source to recheck before submission: https://www.ukgamesfund.com/prototype-fund/

As of 23 May 2026, the Prototype Fund page says Expressions of Interest are open but will be paused soon. Treat this as urgent and prepare the application in one sitting.

## Eligibility Checks

- [ ] UK-based company registered with Companies House.
- [ ] Company has been registered for at least 10 weeks before application.
- [ ] PAYE registration exists and is in use.
- [ ] At least one employee.
- [ ] Fewer than 50 employees.
- [ ] Riddle Quest IP ownership is documented.
- [ ] No gambling elements or mechanics that could be perceived as gambling.
- [ ] Project is not fully launched.
- [ ] Funded work has not already been carried out.
- [ ] Grant request is no more than 50% of the remaining total release cost from the funding point.
- [ ] Subsidy/state-aid history is known and can be declared.
- [ ] Directors can complete identity checks if required.
- [ ] Work can start within the required funding window.

## Evidence To Prepare

- [ ] 2-minute pitch video, browser-playable, below 100MB, not a generic trailer.
- [ ] Playable Android preview build.
- [ ] iOS TestFlight candidate or iOS build evidence.
- [ ] One-page summary.
- [ ] 10-slide pitch deck.
- [ ] Budget and use-of-funds table.
- [ ] Team and contractor plan.
- [ ] Route-to-market plan.
- [ ] Audience validation plan.
- [ ] Proof of community building or beta recruitment.
- [ ] Screenshots or short gameplay clips showing actual game assets.
- [ ] Store privacy/security/data-safety documents.

## Application Claims To Use

- Riddle Quest is an own-IP mobile puzzle game for entertainment.
- The funded work turns an existing beta into a measurable launch-readiness sprint.
- The project has a clear market differentiator: riddle-led deduction layered over a familiar word-game loop.
- The business model is fair freemium: rewarded ads, coin packs, and no-ads IAP without forced spend.
- The grant creates additionality by funding QA, analytics, beta validation, polish, and assets that make later publisher or launch funding more likely.
- The company is not dependent on grant funding alone; publisher, community, platform, and bootstrapped revenue paths remain active.

## If UKGF Prototype Fit Is Weak

Use the Starter Fund / Head Start Pathway if the studio is too early for Prototype Fund expectations.

Current source to recheck before submission: https://starterfund.ukgamesfund.com/head-start/

Positioning for Head Start:

- Solo or early-stage company.
- Has early concept work or demo.
- Needs time to validate audience, team formation, and development/growth plan.
- End goal is to reach Prototype Fund readiness with the same own-IP project.

## Later-Stage Release Funding

Keep the BFI UK Global Screen Fund Video Game Release route warm for launch-campaign support.

Current source to recheck before submission: https://www.bfi.org.uk/get-funding-support/funding-support-international-activity/uk-global-screen-fund/uk-global-screen-fund-ukgsf-international-distribution-video-game-release-funding

As of 23 May 2026:

- Current round opened 14 May 2026.
- Current round closes at 12pm on Friday 14 August 2026.
- Funding supports marketing, promotional, and technical costs for international release activity.
- Application must be submitted no later than eight weeks before the funded activity starts.

## Submission Gate

Do not submit until:

- [ ] The application has a concrete GBP ask and matching cost table.
- [ ] The pitch video shows gameplay, team capability, audience, and route to market.
- [ ] The application explains what funding changes that would not happen otherwise.
- [ ] The project has no unsupported claims about traction, revenue, or retention.
- [ ] All dated source links have been rechecked within 48 hours of submission.
