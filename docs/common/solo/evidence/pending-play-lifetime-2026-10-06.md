# Pending play receipt and retry lifetime — 6 October 2026

This pass prevents paid single-player replies from mutating a replacement account and prevents cancelled retry clocks from sending or acquiring a lease after retirement. It also restores a live delivery interval after React Strict Mode replays a recovered journal. The broader multiplayer and single-player improvement goal remains active.

The production change is in `packages/portal/src/play/game/hooks/useProvisionalPlayRecovery.ts`. Paid Hint, Continue and Skip receipts check the durable journal's account/generation/locale scope before deleting its journal, rolling back gameplay, or reaching generic receipt handling. Delivery has a fresh committed owner, retired during layout cleanup. Interval and timeout callbacks capture that owner and their timer identity; cancelled callbacks cannot borrow a replacement lifetime or clear its retry. Cleanup releases the captured scope's lease and clears timer references so recovery can register again. Ordinary question and profile updates retain delivery, the original request ID, and server authority.

The real call chain reproduced the bugs before these changes: native controls → recovery → native bridge event → actual message listener and router → actual reducer → actual localStorage journal. A Skip rejection from Alice received during Bob's committed layout restored Alice's question and deleted Alice's pending journal. Invoking a retained clock callback during frame removal posted the same paid request again. The corrected maintained harness against the preserved original hook fails **50 of 431 checks in development** and **46 of 431 in production**; development also catches the cancelled interval that prevents Strict Mode recovery from installing a replacement.

The maintained regression is `packages/portal/scripts/verify-pending-play-lifetime-browser.mjs`, registered as `pnpm --dir packages/portal test:pending-play-lifetime:browser`. It drives native controls, warms and awaits the real asynchronous router, and retains callbacks after cancellation to model a queued callback at the layout/passive boundary. It covers all three paid actions with success, rejection and retryable responses across account, generation, locale and logout changes; both renewal and retry timers; unmount; account switching back; ordinary profile/question changes; unchanged ownership; replacement timeouts; and cold journal restoration under Strict Mode. Fixture time is controlled; these results are correctness evidence, not a production network latency benchmark.
