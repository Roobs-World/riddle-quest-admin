# Classic question preparation — 5 October 2026

Hosted Classic victories now quietly prepare one authority-issued successor. A ready Next delivers that question without another provider call or native shared-lock acquisition. Preparation does not publish a question, update a profile, grant rewards, emit a level-start event, or advance the saved game. Explicit Next retains normal request correlation and uses the latest confirmed profile.

The result belongs to the exact delivered predecessor, authenticated account generation, locale, provider and deletion gate. It is consumed once and expires after 15 seconds. Pending, failed, timed-out, expired, mismatched or retired preparation leaves ordinary loading available. Resume and authoritative loads bypass it. Closed gates cannot consume it; gates without a synchronous admission contract disable it. Preparation calls still use the existing coordinated provider operations. Standalone clients do not opt in, and Daily and loss outcomes do not prepare successors.

Commerce callback refreshes previously replaced gameplay wrappers and retired preparation during profile saves. Gameplay and synchronous gate admission now keep a stable identity independently of those callbacks. Current commerce callbacks and products still apply to checkout. Winning milestone guidance now captures explicit victory dismissal, preserving the existing tooltip when the successor arrives in the same render.
