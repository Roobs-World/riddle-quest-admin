# Atomic guess reply validation and accepted draft recovery — 8 October 2026

A successful direct guess reply previously accepted partial feedback by silently dropping invalid cells. It also accepted fractional attempt counters and coerced nonboolean outcome flags. The reproduced old-router browser journey accepted a four-cell row for a five-cell answer and never requested authoritative recovery (`red-browser.log`: expected one reload, observed zero).

The router now validates the complete row before applying it: exact issued answer width, bounded single grapheme cells that remain single cells after locale casing, and one of the three known statuses. Attempt counters must be integers from one through five, or six after a granted Continue. Outcome flags must be booleans; the optional historical `canContinue` field may still be omitted. Malformed successful replies release the pending guess, show the existing translated generic notice, and reload the same riddle and session. They never resend the guess. Valid authoritative outcome flags remain trusted; this is structural validation, not a semantic reconstruction of the server verdict.

That recovery exposed a second defect: a retained checkpoint restored the just-accepted word as the next unsent draft. The reducer now retires that word only when a newer server attempt and canonical accepted history prove it was consumed. Comparison uses the immutable content locale when available. Unrelated drafts, equal counters, and sessions without canonical history retain their draft. Purchased hint cells survive the clear, and the editable cursor moves to the first unlocked cell. Hint and guess validation share the renamed `answerCell` leaf; the old hint-only leaf is removed without a legacy alias.
