# Live safety focus containment

The active multiplayer game now keeps Tab inside the race dialog when its safety panel is collapsed. Shift+Tab from Close reaches the visible safety summary, and Tab from that summary wraps to Close. The shared focus hook now recognizes summary/select/textarea and filters controls that are disabled, inert or not rendered. Expanded Report-to-Close behavior remains correct. No product text or layout changed.

The original selector counted hidden descendants of a collapsed details panel while omitting the summary. Both development and production Chromium reproductions failed the two collapsed-panel checks and passed the expanded control. A fixture-only rectangle/visibility-style candidate still failed: collapsed details descendants retain layout rectangles and visible computed style. The native `checkVisibility({visibilityProperty:true})` predicate rejects them. The method and its cross-browser availability since March 2024 are documented by [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Element/checkVisibility). No optional opacity filtering was introduced, so visible-focus behavior during ordinary fades is retained.
