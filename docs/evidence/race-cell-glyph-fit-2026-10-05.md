The shared board preserves original text, accessible labels, feedback colors, native editing and hit targets. The change applies to race drafts, confirmed history and revealed hints, and also preserves the shared single-player board.

The browser baseline reproduced Hindi conjunct overflow in 48 of 480 measured document states, across Chromium and Firefox, React development/production and small phone, phone, desktop and landscape layouts.
