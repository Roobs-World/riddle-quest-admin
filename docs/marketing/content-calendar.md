# Organic Content Calendar

Last updated: 2026-05-23

## Posting Cadence

- TikTok/Reels/Shorts: 3-5 posts per week during warmup, daily during launch week.
- X/Facebook/Reddit/community posts: 2-4 posts per week, with community rules checked before posting.
- Localization: repost the best-performing English formats in the top 2-3 responding languages.

## Reusable Captions

- "Riddle lovers, this one is for you. Five guesses. One clue. No random words."
- "If Wordle and a brain teaser had a daily challenge, it would feel like this."
- "The answer is obvious after you see it. Until then? Chaos."
- "Green means right letter, right spot. Yellow means the letter is hiding somewhere else."
- "Start with the clue, then let the colors do the work."

## Community Posting Targets

- Word puzzle groups.
- Riddle and brain teaser communities.
- Indie game communities.
- Language-learning communities for localized tests.
- Cozy/casual mobile game groups.

Always adapt posts to community rules and avoid drive-by promotion. Lead with a playable riddle or a beta feedback request.
