# 90-Day Launch Calendar

Last updated: 2026-05-23

## Weeks 1-2: Foundation

Goal: make every traffic destination credible before outreach or paid tests begin.

| Workstream        | Deliverable                                                             | Owner               | Done When                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Store setup       | App Store and Google Play metadata drafted from `store-metadata-aso.md` | Marketing/Product   | Title, subtitle/short description, long description, keywords, screenshots, preview notes, and privacy claims are ready for console entry |
| Creative          | Screenshot and video shot list from `creative-production-brief.md`      | Design/Marketing    | 5-8 screenshots and 1 preview video storyboard exist for the default English listing                                                      |
| Landing           | Publish `prelaunch-landing-page.html` or adapt it into the main site    | Web/Marketing       | Waitlist or pre-registration links are live and trackable                                                                                 |
| Tracking          | Configure link taxonomy from `tracking-and-kpis.md`                     | Marketing/Analytics | Campaign URLs include source, medium, campaign, content, and language                                                                     |
| Product readiness | Confirm beta build, privacy disclosures, and review-sensitive copy      | Product/Release     | TestFlight/internal testing links, store privacy drafts, and support email are ready                                                      |

## Weeks 3-4: Beta And Content Warmup

Goal: collect proof, sharpen creative, and recruit early advocates before public launch.

| Workstream       | Deliverable                                       | Owner               | Done When                                                                               |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Beta             | Recruit 100-300 beta testers                      | Marketing/Community | Testers come from puzzle, word game, brain teaser, language, and indie game communities |
| Organic social   | Post 3-5 short videos per week                    | Marketing           | Posts use real riddles, readable captions, and clear "Play Riddle Quest" end cards      |
| Creator outreach | Contact 30-50 micro-creators                      | Marketing           | Creators are logged with status, fee, audience, language, and link code                 |
| Paid validation  | Spend USD 20-50/day only after listings are ready | Marketing           | At least 3 creative angles are tested with separate links                               |
| Feedback loop    | Weekly beta review                                | Product/Marketing   | Record friction, favorite moments, confusing copy, and best shareable wins              |

## Weeks 5-8: Launch

Goal: concentrate attention around a repeatable daily challenge campaign.

| Workstream       | Deliverable                          | Owner     | Done When                                                                                |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Campaign         | Run the 7-Day Riddle Quest Challenge | Marketing | Daily posts, creator prompts, and store links are live for 7 consecutive days            |
| PR               | Send launch note and press kit       | Marketing | Mobile game, puzzle, education, indie, and local press lists receive personalized emails |
| Creators         | Publish first creator wave           | Marketing | 10-20 creator posts go live with tracked links                                           |
| Store conversion | Route channels to matching pages     | Marketing | Apple custom product pages and Google custom store listings map to campaign themes       |
| Ratings          | Prompt after positive moments        | Product   | Review prompt is tied to win, streak, achievement, or day-3 return                       |

## Weeks 9-12: Optimize And Scale

Goal: fund what works and stop what only looks busy.

| Workstream       | Deliverable                        | Owner                  | Done When                                                                                                   |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ASO tests        | Run one asset/copy test at a time  | Marketing              | Icon, first screenshot, short description, or video tests run long enough to cover weekday/weekend behavior |
| Channel scale    | Increase spend on winning channels | Marketing              | Budget shifts toward retained-user cost, not just low CPI                                                   |
| Localization     | Expand top 2-3 languages           | Marketing/Localization | Store pages, social hooks, and creator briefs are localized for the best responders                         |
| Creative refresh | Ship weekly new assets             | Design/Marketing       | New videos cover riddle solves, failure reveals, streaks, leaderboard, and hint moments                     |
| Reporting        | Weekly KPI readout                 | Marketing/Product      | Report includes conversion, CPI, D1/D7 retention, ratings, reviews, and creative winners                    |

## Weekly Review Agenda

1. What was shipped?
2. What drove installs?
3. What drove retained users?
4. Which creative or keyword failed cleanly?
5. What is the next test, and what will we stop doing?
