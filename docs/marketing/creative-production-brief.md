# Creative Production Brief

Last updated: 2026-05-23

## Creative Pillars

1. Riddle-first gameplay: the clue is the hook.
2. Deduction loop: guesses create color feedback.
3. Daily habit: streaks, daily challenge, shareable wins.
4. Progression: Classic Mode, coins, hints, achievements, leaderboard.
5. Fair free play: optional hints and Jest purchases.

## App Preview Video

Length: 15-30 seconds.

Structure:

1. 0-3s: show a riddle hook on screen.
2. 3-8s: enter a guess and reveal color feedback.
3. 8-14s: solve or nearly solve the puzzle.
4. 14-20s: show victory, coins, streak, or achievement.
5. 20-30s: show Daily Challenge and Classic Mode, end with "Play Riddle Quest."

## Static Puzzle Posts

Create 20 static posts using the format "Can you solve this?"

Template:

- Header: "Can you solve this?"
- Body: one short riddle.
- Footer: "Answer in Riddle Quest."
- Variant: add 5 empty letter boxes if the answer length is known.

Post bank:

1. I have keys but no locks. What am I?
2. I speak without a mouth and hear without ears. What am I?
3. The more you take, the more you leave behind. What am I?
4. What gets wetter the more it dries?
5. What has a neck but no head?
6. What has hands but cannot clap?
7. What has many teeth but cannot bite?
8. What comes once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a thousand years?
9. What can travel around the world while staying in a corner?
10. What has words but never speaks?
11. What breaks before you use it?
12. What has a face and two hands but no arms or legs?
13. What can fill a room but takes up no space?
14. What has branches but no fruit, trunk, or leaves?
15. What has a ring but no finger?
16. What begins with T, ends with T, and has T inside?
17. What has one eye but cannot see?
18. What has cities but no houses?
19. What can you catch but not throw?
20. What goes up but never comes down?

## Production Rules

- Use real gameplay captures where possible.
- Keep captions large enough for narrow social feeds.
- Avoid tiny UI text in paid ads.
- Show the riddle by second 2 in video.
- End every asset with one clear CTA: "Play Riddle Quest" or "Join the 7-Day Challenge."
- Localized assets must use real translated UI and game-page copy, not only translated captions.
