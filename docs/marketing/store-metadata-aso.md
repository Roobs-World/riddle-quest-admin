### Full Description

Riddle Quest is a daily word puzzle game where every answer starts with a clue.

Read the riddle, make your guess, and use color feedback to narrow down the answer. Green means the letter is correct and in the right place, yellow means the letter appears somewhere else, and red means it is not in the answer.

Play one daily challenge for a quick brain-training habit, or continue through Classic Mode for a longer puzzle quest. Earn coins, use hints when you get stuck, build streaks, unlock achievements, and compare your progress on the leaderboard.

Features:

- Riddle-based word puzzle gameplay
- Daily Challenge mode
- Classic Mode with progressive levels
- Wordle-style color feedback
- Hints, coins, streaks, stats, and achievements
- Leaderboards and shareable victories
- Multilingual support
- Fair free play with optional rewards and purchases

If you like Wordle, crosswords, riddles, brain teasers, or quick daily puzzle games, Riddle Quest gives you a fresh mystery to solve every day.

## Compliance Notes

- Do not imply affiliation with Wordle.
- Use "Wordle-style" only where store policy and review judgment allow; keep primary copy centered on Riddle Quest.
- Avoid claiming "millions of players" until verified.
- Keep privacy, ads, IAP, and tracking claims aligned with `docs/app-store-privacy-disclosures.md` and `docs/google-play-data-safety.md`.
